# Document Textures

Place textures in `public/textures/documents/`. These are loaded at runtime by the renderer.

Suggested files:

- public/textures/documents/paper_plain_1.png — Lightly aged plain paper with defined edges (PNG)
- public/textures/documents/paper_plain_2.png — Plain paper with subtle fibers
- public/textures/documents/paper_plain_3.png — Plain paper variant
- public/textures/documents/security_paper_1.png — Security/microprint paper for legal forms
- public/textures/documents/letterhead_dept.png — Department letterhead header strip; include agency seal/badge on the left, department name and division centered/right, subtle divider line across the page top
- public/textures/documents/letterhead_me.png — Medical examiner letterhead; caduceus/ME seal left, "Office of the Medical Examiner" text, contact block right-aligned
- public/textures/documents/letterhead_corp.png — Corporate memo header; company logo left, company name and address line, light bottom rule for separation
- public/textures/documents/letterhead_generic.png — Generic letterhead header strip; neutral styling with simple rule line, no logos
- public/textures/documents/notary_stamp.png — Notary/judge stamp overlay
- public/textures/documents/form_grid.png — Light grid/table guide
- public/textures/documents/grid_paper.png — Grid paper for sketches
- public/textures/documents/pencil_marks.png — Pencil stroke overlays
- public/textures/documents/newsprint.png — Newspaper texture
- public/textures/documents/torn_edge.png — Torn/rough edge mask
- public/textures/documents/ticket_paper.png — Ticket stock texture
- public/textures/documents/barcode_area.png — Barcode/QR strip placeholder
- public/textures/documents/logo_placeholder.png — Placeholder logo (bank/company)
- public/textures/documents/lined_paper.png — Blue ruled lines texture
- public/textures/documents/ink_bleed.png — Ink bleed overlay


Possible files types
Case Files

Case File Cover: Title block, case number, investigators, dates.
Investigation Summary Report: Narrative sections, findings, recommendations.
Timeline Sheet: Chronological events with timestamps and sources.
Evidence Index: Itemized list with tags, locations, and status.
Task Checklist: Assigned tasks, due dates, completion boxes.
Witness/Subject Profiles: Photo area, identifiers, notes.
Law & Legal

Search Warrant: Court header, affidavit, property list, judge signature.
Subpoena: Recipient, required materials/testimony, court info.
Affidavit/Sworn Statement: Oath language, signature blocks, notary.
Miranda Waiver/Consent Form: Checkboxes, signature lines, disclaimers.
Property Consent Form: Owner info, address, items, authorization.
Warrant Return/Inventory: Items seized, serials, officer signature.
Police & Operations

Incident Report: Narrative, classification, officers, location, time.
Arrest/Booking Sheet: Charges, custody info, personal details.
Rap Sheet (Criminal History): Offenses, dates, dispositions.
Dispatch Log: Call times, units, statuses (10-codes).
Patrol Memo/Shift Notes: Briefs, BOLOs, assignments.
Evidence Tag/Label: Item ID, date/time, collector, location.
Forensics & Lab

Lab Submission Form: Item details, requested analyses, chain start.
Chain-of-Custody Log: Transfers, dates/times, signatures.
DNA Report: Loci table, match probabilities, conclusions.
Gel Electrophoresis Sheet: Lane map, band annotations, notes.
Fingerprint Card (Ink): Ten-print boxes, identifiers.
Latent Print Lift Card: Lift patch area, case ID, notes.
AFIS Match Summary: Candidate list, score, match details.
Ballistics Report: Weapon, ammo, striae comparison, conclusions.
Toxicology Report: Substances, levels, reference ranges.
Spectrograph Output Page: Chart area, peak annotations, parameters.
Medical & EMS

Autopsy Report: External/internal findings, tox, cause/manner.
EMS Run Sheet: Patient vitals, scene notes, interventions.
Medical Record Extract: Visits, diagnoses, prescriptions.
Pathology Summary: Histology notes, diagnosis.
Financial & Records

Bank Statement: Transactions, balances, account details.
Wire Transfer Receipt: Sender/receiver, amount, reference.
Credit Card Statement: Itemized charges, merchant info.
Insurance Policy/Claim: Coverage, claim details, adjuster notes.
Utility Bill: Service address, billing period, usage.
Communications & Digital

Phone Call Log: Dialed/received, durations, contact names.
911 Transcript: Caller/dispatcher lines, timestamps.
SMS/Chat Log: Message bubbles, metadata.
Email Printout: Headers, body, attachments list.
Social Media Post: Post card with handle, timestamp, reactions.
Device Forensics Summary: Artifacts found, locations, hash values.
Travel & Logistics

Airline Boarding Pass/Itinerary: Flight segment blocks, QR area.
Hotel Receipt: Stay dates, room charges, payments.
Shipping/Courier Manifest: Tracking numbers, sender/recipient, contents.
Vehicle Registration: VIN, owner, plate, expiration.
Repair Invoice: Parts/labor, shop info, totals.
Media & Public

News Article/Clipping: Headline, byline, column layout.
Missing Person Flyer: Photo, descriptors, contact info, reward.
Press Release: Agency header, statement sections.
Brochure/Flyer: Tri-fold or single, key highlights.
Maps & Scene Artifacts

Crime Scene Sketch: Site plan, legend, measurements.
Floor Plan: Rooms, labels, key features.
Annotated Map: Route, pins, timestamps, notes.
Photo Print with Caption: Photo area, caption, date, photographer.
CCTV Snapshot Sheet: Frames, timestamp overlays, camera ID.
Personal & Misc

Handwritten Note: Lined/grid paper, handwriting texture.
Notebook Page: Spiral margin, date header.
Index Card: Ruled card with category header.
Sticky Note: Small square, adhesive edge, scribbles.
Business Card: Name, role, contact, logo placement.
Envelope Front: Address blocks, stamps, postmarks.
Postcard: Photo front, message/address back.
Appointment Card: Date/time, location, contact.
Calendar Page: Month grid, highlighted events.
To-Do List: Checkboxes, priorities, owner.
Administrative & Corporate

Internal Memo: Letterhead, from/to/subject, body.
Meeting Minutes: Attendees, agenda, decisions, actions.
HR File Memo: Employee, incident, supervisor notes.
School Transcript/Attendance: Courses, grades, absences.
Employment Record: Position, dates, supervisor, reviews.
Audio & Transcripts

Interview Transcript: Q/A formatting, speaker labels, timestamps.
Audio Log Summary: File names, durations, notes.
Template/Texture Bases

Letterhead Page: Agency/corporate header/footer elements.
Plain Paper (A4/Letter): Subtle fiber texture, light aging.
Notebook/Lined/Grid: Rule lines, margins, punch holes.
Index/Sticky Note: Cardboard/fiber or sticky sheen.
Photo Paper: Glossy/semi-gloss with slight curl.
Envelope/Postcard: Kraft texture, crease marks.
Labels/Tags: Adhesive label sheets, stringed tags.
Security Paper: Watermarks/microprint for official forms.
